# ask: How should a consulting team define success, both with the client and within the team itself?

- mode: ask · execution: local · model: `gemma4:e2b-it-qat`
- time: 2026-09-29T21:48:13 · status: **cited** · decided by: model

## Answer

The team should define success by setting shared goals that align with individual aims and can be evaluated and measured over time to determine if success criteria are met [S2]. Team goals should be challenging, consequential, and measurable [S4].

For the client project, potential categories for the main objective include improved revenue, profitability, capabilities, customer intimacy, future optionality, culture, sustainability, and cost structure, among others [S3]. Success for the project can be defined by what success looks like at the end of the project [S3].

Within the team, team goals should be developed based on individual motivations, ensuring they align with individual aims, and the team must ensure each member fully understands and believes in the goals, and that team members are energized by them [S4]. The team needs to determine how they will know if they have achieved these goals [S4].

> Part I:  Individual Motivations, Strengths & Development Goals
