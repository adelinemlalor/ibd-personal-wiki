# Team Roles and Expectations

How IBD teams are made up and what the program expects of team members and team leads.

## Details by source

<!-- wiki:from:src-ibd-info-session-spring-2026-slides:start -->
### From [[IBD Program Info Session]]
- Each team has 4 members, including a Team Lead, and 1 Faculty Mentor. ([[raw/IBD Info Session Spring 2026_Slides.pdf#page=7|IBD Info Session Spring 2026_Slides.pdf p.7]])
- **Team members:** 8–10 hours/week including class time and client calls; timely, high-quality output; engagement throughout the semester; completing all client-related tasks; cooperating with the team lead and members; helping teammates improve their work. ([[raw/IBD Info Session Spring 2026_Slides.pdf#page=8|IBD Info Session Spring 2026_Slides.pdf p.8]])
- **Team leads:** 10–12 hours/week; building the team schedule and managing progress; making sure tasks are done on time; coaching team members; communicating with the team, faculty mentor, IBD staff, and clients; coordinating travel logistics; new this year, 1 additional unit. ([[raw/IBD Info Session Spring 2026_Slides.pdf#page=8|IBD Info Session Spring 2026_Slides.pdf p.8]])
