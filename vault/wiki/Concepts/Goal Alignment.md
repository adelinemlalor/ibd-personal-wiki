# Goal Alignment

The process of developing team goals that align with individual motivations and ensuring mutual understanding and belief.

## Details by source

<!-- wiki:from:src-teams-haas-collaborative-plan:start -->
### From [[Teams@Haas Collaborative Plan]]
- Team goals should be challenging, consequential, and measurable.
- Team goals must align with individual aims.
- Team members must fully understand and believe in the goals.
