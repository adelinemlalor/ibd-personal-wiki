# Teams@Haas Collaborative Plan

A tool designed to guide a team discussion for designing a team to effectively execute work and foster a safe environment for exploration and growth.

## Summary

The Teams@Haas Collaborative Plan is a tool designed to guide a team discussion for designing a team to effectively execute work and foster a safe environment for exploration and growth. It supports developing a collective understanding of individual goals, shared purpose, norms, and structures to coordinate work and maintain accountability.

## Key points

<!-- wiki:keypoints:start -->
- Part I: Individual Motivations, Strengths & Development Goals requires discussing each member’s prior work experience, professional goals, and core areas where they hope to evolve as a leader. — [[raw/Teams@Haas Collaborative Plan .docx|Teams@Haas Collaborative Plan .docx]]
- Team goals should be challenging, consequential, and measurable. — [[raw/Teams@Haas Collaborative Plan .docx|Teams@Haas Collaborative Plan .docx]]
- Consider norms that will help cultivate the five key elements of a strong team collaborative climate: 1) Balanced Communication, 2) Reflexivity, 3) Conflict Management, 4) Socio-Emotional Intelligence & 5) Psychological Safety. — [[raw/Teams@Haas Collaborative Plan .docx|Teams@Haas Collaborative Plan .docx]]
- Team Meetings require discussion on purpose, frequency, time, location, duration, meeting roles (Weekly Agendas & Minutes, Time Keeper, Scribe, etc.), and protocols. — [[raw/Teams@Haas Collaborative Plan .docx|Teams@Haas Collaborative Plan .docx]]
