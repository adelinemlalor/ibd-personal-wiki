# IBD Client Questionnaire

Outlines the structure for an IBD Client Questionnaire covering various strategic and contextual areas.

## Summary

This document outlines a questionnaire structure for an IBD Client Questionnaire. The questionnaire covers strategic challenges, project objectives, market analysis, customer insights, organizational context, and engagement design. It serves as a framework for gathering necessary information from a client.
